How future annual data should be added

GitHub Pages cannot reliably read and interpret a newly uploaded PDF by itself. The app therefore keeps a structured JavaScript data block inside index.html. When UC releases a new annual PDF, the values must first be reviewed and transcribed into one new year object.

  1. Upload the new UC California-resident first-year PDF for review.
  2. Extract the nine campus rows: applications, admits, admit rate, GPA range, A-G range, and honors range.
  3. Add a new year object to ADMISSIONS, preserving the published snapshot date.
  4. Add the new year to the year selectors. No visual redesign or scoring-code rewrite should be necessary.
  5. Replace the existing GitHub index.html with the revised file.

Why this architecture: the source can remain a PDF, but the browser still needs clean structured values to power filters, charts, comparisons, and the student-outlook calculation. The annual PDF remains the authoritative source; the embedded data object is the app-readable transcription.

Sources: University of California, Office of the President, Information Center, annual California-resident first-year/freshman application-data counselor snapshots for Fall 2023, Fall 2024, and Fall 2025. GPA is honors-weighted and capped at eight honors-level semesters. Course-count ranges are semesters or semester-equivalents. *Enrollment counts for 2023 and 2024 are taken from the following year's snapshot; Fall 2025 enrollment was not reported in the provided 2025 snapshot. This application is for exploration and counseling support and does not represent UC admission probabilities or guarantees.
